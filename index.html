Breakpoint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Breakpoint does not collect any information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, record, sell, share or send any personal data, usage data or any other information.

What is saved on your device

The game saves your progress (upgrades, settings and key choices) in your browser's local storage, on your own computer. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Removing the extension or clearing your browsing data deletes it.

Permissions and the network

The extension asks for no special permissions. It makes no network requests, has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it needs no account.

Third parties

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the extension's store listing before any data is collected.
